    Learn the Pythagorean theorem in 5 minutes

    By the end of this mini-lesson, you'll know how to find a missing side of a right triangle and be ready for a 3-question quiz.

    Minute 1: Understand the triangle

    A right triangle is a triangle with one 90° angle.

    • aaa and bbb are the two sides that meet at the right angle.

    • ccc is the hypotenuse — the longest side, opposite the right angle.

    The Pythagorean theorem tells us how these three side lengths relate.

    Minute 2: Learn the rule

    Here's the key idea: if you square the length of each shorter side and add them together, you get the square of the hypotenuse.

    Remember: square each shorter side, add, then find the square root to get the hypotenuse.

    • Squaring a number means multiplying it by itself: 32=3×3=93^2=3\times3=932=3×3=9.

    • The square root reverses squaring: 25=5\sqrt{25}=525​=5.

    Minute 4: Find a missing shorter side

    What if you know the hypotenuse is 13 and one shorter side is 5?

    1. Square the hypotenuse: 132=16913^2=169132=169.

    2. Square the known shorter side: 52=255^2=2552=25.

    3. Subtract: 169−25=144169-25=144169−25=144.

    4. Take the square root: 144=12\sqrt{144}=12144​=12.

    The missing side is 12.

    The trick: When finding a shorter side, subtract the known shorter side's square from the hypotenuse's square before taking the square root.

    Minute 5: Remember when to use it

    Use the theorem when:

    • You have a right triangle.

    • You know two side lengths and need the third.

    • You need to calculate a diagonal, such as across a rectangular floor or screen.

    Quick memory trick: Square, add or subtract, square root.
